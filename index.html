How to prepare your files

  1. In your PPTX template, type placeholders exactly as {{FirstName}}, {{PMI_ID}} etc. wherever you want a value inserted — matching your spreadsheet's column headers (spaces/underscores/case don't matter).
  2. Your CSV/XLSX must have a header row. "First name" and "Second name" columns are used for the output filename.
  3. Upload both files below and generate.
